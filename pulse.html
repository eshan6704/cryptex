Risk rule (lot fixed at 0.01): with $3 max loss and $0.15 commission, net risk is $2.85. XAUUSD (standard 100-oz lot, 0.01 lot = 1oz, $1 move = $1 P/L) → SL distance ≈ $2.85 price move. XAUUSD mini (same quote feed as XAUUSD, but margin and P/L are both ÷5, so $1 move = $0.20 P/L) → SL distance ≈ $14.25 price move (2.85 × 5). BTCUSDT (0.01 lot, $1 move = $0.01 P/L) → SL distance ≈ $285 price move. Either way a full SL hit is $2.85 gross + $0.15 commission = $3.00 maximum loss — or set "Manual SL distance override" above to use a fixed price distance instead of the $-risk calculation. TP is trailing, not a fixed target: it tracks the best price reached since entry ("TP" column) and exits once price gives back the configured "Trailing TP giveback %" (default 2.5%) from that peak — but only once net profit (after fees) is at least the "Min profit before momentum exit" amount, so a shallow pullback right after entry won't close the trade early. The same profit floor also gates the imbalance-based momentum-reversal exit. "Max running trades" caps concurrent open positions. Daily trading stops after realized losses reach 10% of the starting demo balance.
